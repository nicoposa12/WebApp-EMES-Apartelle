INTRODUCTION
Project Context
Siargao Island, widely recognized as the Surfing Capital of the Philippines, has experienced rapid growth as a global tourist destination. With the continuous recovery of the tourism industry and the increasing number of local and international travelers, the demand for reliable and efficient accommodation services has significantly increased. In recent years, the island has welcomed hundreds of thousands of visitors annually, particularly in the municipality of General Luna, which serves as the center of tourism activities.

As tourism continues to grow, hospitality establishments are challenged to improve their operational efficiency and service quality. Many small and medium accommodations still rely on manual processes such as handwritten reservation logs, manual billing, and paper-based guest records. These traditional methods often lead to operational inefficiencies, including booking conflicts, delayed responses to guest inquiries, difficulty in monitoring room availability, and inaccurate financial documentation.

EME’s Apartelle is one of the accommodation providers operating in the area that currently utilizes manual processes for reservations, guest monitoring, and billing. While this method was manageable during periods of lower tourist activity, the increasing volume of visitors has made it difficult to efficiently handle reservations and manage guest records.

To address these challenges, this study proposes the development of EME’s Apartelle Digital Transformation: An Automated Booking and Billing Management System with Chatbot-Assisted Guest Support. The system is designed to modernize the operational management of the apartelle through a centralized web-based platform. It integrates automated booking management, billing processing, guest check-in and check-out monitoring, and administrative reporting.

The proposed system also incorporates modern technological features such as a chatbot for guest assistance, live chat communication, real-time notifications, secure online payment integration, and One-Time Password (OTP) authentication for user login security. Through these innovations, the system aims to improve operational efficiency, reduce manual errors, and provide a more convenient booking experience for guests.

Ultimately, the implementation of a digital management system will enable EME’s Apartelle to keep pace with the rapid growth of tourism in Siargao Island while providing better service quality and improved management of hospitality operations.

Purpose and Description
The primary purpose of the Automated Booking and Billing Management System for EME’s Apartelle is to modernize and enhance the management of accommodation services through a digital platform that integrates reservation handling, guest monitoring, billing, and communication processes.

The system provides an efficient interface that allows guests to browse available rooms, check real-time availability, and reserve accommodations through an online platform. At the same time, the system allows the management to efficiently monitor bookings, manage guest check-ins and check-outs, track room occupancy, and generate automated billing records.

One of the major features of the system is its real-time booking management capability, which ensures that room availability is updated instantly. This reduces the possibility of double bookings and improves the accuracy of reservation records. Additionally, the system incorporates One-Time Password (OTP) authentication to ensure secure login access for both guests and administrators.

The system also includes a chatbot capable of answering frequently asked questions and assisting guests with booking inquiries at any time. A live chat feature is also integrated to allow direct communication between guests and apartelle staff when more personalized assistance is required.

To further enhance operational efficiency, the system provides automated billing generation, online payment integration, and administrative reporting tools that allow management to monitor business performance, track occupancy rates, and analyze booking trends.

By implementing this digital management platform, EME’s Apartelle can significantly improve its operational workflow, enhance guest satisfaction, and strengthen its competitiveness within the rapidly growing tourism industry of Siargao Island.

In addition, the system includes an amenities management feature that allows guests to view available facilities and services offered by the apartelle, such as Wi-Fi access, air-conditioning, television, kitchen utilities, and other room or property amenities. Guests can use these details to make more informed booking decisions, while administrators can easily manage and update amenity listings for each room. This feature enhances transparency, improves guest satisfaction, and aligns the platform with modern hospitality booking standards.

Scope and Limitations
Scope
This study focuses on the development of a web-based booking and billing management system designed to improve the operational management of EME’s Apartelle. The system includes the following functionalities:
1.	The system will provide online room booking where guests can check room availability, select preferred dates, and reserve rooms.
2.	The system will display of room information including photos, room types, and pricing details.
3.	The system will ensure secure access through user account login for both guests and administrators, reinforced by One-Time Password (OTP) verification.
4.	The system will automate billing generation and real-time notifications for booking confirmations, payment status, and reservation updates.
5.	The system will feature an administrative dashboard for monitoring check-in/out schedules, tracking occupancy, and managing room-specific amenities.
6.	The system will deploy chatbot assistance and live chat functionality to provide immediate responses to inquiries and facilitate direct guest-staff communication.
7.	The system will display room amenities, including facilities such as Wi-Fi, air-conditioning, private bathrooms, televisions, and other services.
Limitations
The system requires a stable internet connection to function effectively.
1.	The system operates only as a web-based platform and does not function as a standalone offline mobile application.
2.	Online payment processing depends on third-party payment gateways, and the system developers are not responsible for payment delays caused by banking or network issues.
3.	The chatbot is limited to pre-programmed responses for frequently asked questions and cannot replace human decision-making for complex requests.
4.	The system does not include hardware integration such as automated room access systems or energy control devices.
5.	OTP verification relies on the telecommunication provider’s SMS delivery, which may occasionally experience delays.
6.	All transactions and financial reports are processed using Philippine Peso (PHP) and do not support multi-currency conversion.
 
Review of Related Literature/Studies/Systems
The digital transformation of hospitality and accommodation services has become increasingly crucial as the industry responds to pressure from rising traveler expectations and the limitations of traditional, manual operations. Research indicates that manual reservation and billing systems are prone to errors, inefficiencies, and poor customer service outcomes, particularly in high demand tourist destinations (Gaur, 2024).

Recent literature highlights the widespread adoption of online hotel reservation systems as an effective response to these challenges. Vaishnavi, Kumar, & Joshi (2025) found that web based booking platforms allow guests to check availability, make reservations, and receive immediate confirmation, simultaneously reducing administrative workload and improving transparency for both guests and hotel staff. These systems also prevent double bookings and provide automated notifications, demonstrating the operational efficiency gains of digital platforms over manual methods.

In addition to reservation systems, the integration of chatbots and conversational agents within hospitality services has emerged as a significant trend. Conversational AI tools are employed not just for booking support, but as guest engagement platforms offering real time responses and improving overall service quality. A recent bibliometric review indicates that chatbot applications in tourism and hospitality have grown substantially, with leading global brands deploying chatbots that handle customer queries, assist in reservations, and enhance user interaction through natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning techniques (Aransyah, Hermanto, Muftiadi, & Oktadiana, 2026; Yılmaz & Şahin, 2024).

Artificial intelligence (AI) powered chatbots have also been shown to significantly improve operational workflows by providing 24/7 support, reducing staff workloads, and increasing direct bookings. Industry reports note that hospitality chatbots can handle a large portion of guest inquiries and facilitate upselling opportunities, thereby enhancing both efficiency and revenue generation (Napänkangas, 2026).

Experimental research further supports the importance of chatbots in the booking process. A 2026 study on AI in tourism booking contexts found that, while human agents may be preferred in some situations, chatbots nonetheless offer competitive booking intentions and operational advantages—suggesting that guest acceptance of automated support continues to grow as technology sophistication improves (Sukthankar, Fernandes, Gaonkar, & Shetye, 2026).

Furthermore, automation in billing and financial processes is becoming a cornerstone of modern hospitality management. In Malaysia’s hospitality industry, e invoicing and real-time billing solutions powered by AI have significantly reduced manual errors, accelerated checkout times, and increased financial transparency. These advancements align with broader digital transformation trends that emphasize real-time data accuracy and personalized guest experiences (Chen, Konar, & Kumar, 2024).

METHODOLOGY
Technical Background
The following technologies are suggested for the development and deployment of the system:
•	Web Application Framework: The EME’s Apartelle system is being developed as a modern web application using Next.js 14 and Node.js. This framework was chosen to ensure a high-performance, SEO-friendly platform for both guests and administrators. Next.js 14, built on the React library, utilizes Server-Side Rendering (SSR) and Static Site Generation (SSG), which ensures that room catalogs and booking pages load almost instantly. Its component-based architecture allows for a modular development process, making it easier to manage the complex logic of the integrated chatbot and the real-time booking engine. 
Node.js serves as the backend runtime environment. Its non-blocking, event-driven I/O model is highly efficient for handling the concurrent traffic typical of a hotel booking site, ensuring that the system remains responsive even when multiple guests are inquiring via the chatbot or checking room availability simultaneously.

•	Responsive Web Design: To eliminate the need for a separate mobile application, the system is engineered with a "Mobile-First" responsive design. Using CSS media queries and flexible grid layouts, the website automatically adjusts its interface to provide an optimal viewing experience on smartphones, tablets, laptops, and desktops. This ensures that guests can easily navigate the apartelle’s features, view high-quality room images, and complete reservations using any device with an internet connection, providing maximum convenience without requiring a download.
•	Database Management System: The project utilizes MongoDB as its primary database, paired with Prisma as the Object-Relational Mapper (ORM). MongoDB is a document-oriented NoSQL database that offers the flexibility needed to store structured data (like room rates and guest profiles) alongside semi-structured data (such as chat logs from the automated assistant).
Prisma provides a type-safe database client that simplifies data modeling and migrations. It ensures that the connection between the web application and the database is secure and efficient, reducing the risk of data inconsistencies during peak booking periods.

•	Styling and Design System: To create a professional and inviting "hospitality" aesthetic, the system is styled using Tailwind CSS and Shadcn/UI. Tailwind CSS allows for rapid, utility-based styling that maintains a consistent look across all pages. Shadcn/UI contributes a library of accessible, pre-designed components such as the date-picker for check-in dates, interactive modals for room previews, and the floating chat widget giving the website a polished, industry-standard feel.

•	Hosting and Domain Deployment: For its official launch, the platform will be hosted on DigitalOcean with domain management through GoDaddy. DigitalOcean offers a robust cloud infrastructure (Droplets) that guarantees high uptime, which is critical for a business that accepts 24/7 online reservations. GoDaddy will provide the official web address for EME’s Apartelle, ensuring the site is easily searchable and recognizable to local and international tourists.

User information and booking records must be securely stored and protected against unauthorized access. Data retention policies shall be implemented, and all transactions must maintain an accurate and secure audit trail.





Design of Software, System, Product, and/or Processes

Waterfall Development Methodology
The development of EME’s Apartelle Online Booking and Management System follows the Waterfall Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) model. This approach is selected for its structured and sequential process, ensuring that each phase is completed thoroughly before moving to the next. It is well-suited for projects with clearly defined requirements and provides organized documentation throughout development.

1. Requirements
Identify the needs of the apartelle by analyzing current booking challenges such as manual processes, lack of automation, and inefficient customer interaction. Define system requirements including online booking, real-time availability tracking, chatbot integration, and administrative controls. Establish both functional and non-functional requirements for guests, staff, and administrators.

2. Design

3. Implementation

4. Testing

5. Deployment

6. Maintenance
Monitor system performance and resolve any issues after deployment. Apply updates and improvements based on user feedback. Ensure regular data backups, security updates, and system enhancements to maintain reliability and efficiency over time.
